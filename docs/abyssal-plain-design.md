# The Abyssal Plain

**Status: built.**

A fully underwater dimension, `strata:abyssal_plain`, in the spirit of Subnautica: water everywhere, no surface, a deep seafloor to explore, and bases you have to engineer before you can breathe in them.

## 1. Design decisions
- **No surface.** A rock ceiling sits above the water; there is nowhere to swim up to.
- **Core mod: Create: Deep Seas** (beta; boots clean on the test server with our Create Aeronautics 1.3.2 and Sable 2.0.6). It provides the gameplay systems: physics submarines, water pressure on divers and hulls, the copper diving suit, airlocks (Decompression Chamber), Electrolyzer + Oxygen Diffuser, the Seaglide. Its own Abyss creatures, vents and lianas are **switched off in released builds** ("content disabled in production"), so the dimension's life comes from other mods (section 5).
- "Deeper Oceans" (Deep Seas' Overworld ocean reshaping) stays off; `config/create_submarine-common.toml` ships with it off.

## 2. Getting there
- **Arrival: the Lifepod, on a shelf.** With no surface, a player stepping through the portal would drown at once, and most of the sea floor is deeper than plain gear survives. So nobody arrives where the portal would put them: the first trip from a place finds the nearest Abyssal Shelf and a **Lifepod** is set down there, as if it had come down from above and hit the shelf (a short gouge and some wreckage lie behind it). Later trips from around the same place, and anyone else whose nearest shelf it is, arrive in the same pod. The pod is an upright capsule: a round white hull nine blocks across with an orange stripe, an inflated orange flotation collar around its base, a rounded shoulder and cap with a top hatch, a mast with a beacon, portholes on three sides and LIFEPOD stencilled on the front. It stands on four struts, so the moon pool in the middle of the deck opens onto clear water. Inside: the **Return to Surface** console with the supply chest (a few starter supplies, nothing that skips progression) and a crafting table, seats along both walls, lockers and a radio. Every pod block is unbreakable; no flood or oxygen rule touches it. Textures: `tools/build_lifepod.py`.
- **Going home: the console, not a portal.** The pod has no return portal: BBL's return link sent a playtester to a new portal in a cave instead of their own. While a player is in the Overworld, their last spot on the ground outside any portal is kept; the console puts them back there, facing away from the portal (world spawn if nothing is kept).
- **Vampires drown here:** Vampirism normally refills a vampire's air every tick; in the Abyssal Plain `startup_scripts/abyssal_vampire_air.js` keeps their own air count (drains underwater, drowning damage at empty; Water Breathing, Conduit power and diving helmets still work). Elsewhere vampires keep the benefit.
- **Portal:** a frame of **dark prismarine** lit with the **Tidecaller**: a Conduit in the middle, a Deep Seas Ballast Tank above and below it, prismarine crystals down both sides (`P B P / P C P / P B P`). The Conduit needs a Heart of the Sea (buried treasure) and nautilus shells; the ballast tanks mean Deep Seas progress first. The Conduit is an ingredient, not the lighter, because a Conduit is a placeable block. Same BBL Portals setup as the Pokémon world and the Hollow. **Look and sound:** its own animated "marine snow" texture (near-black water, flecks drifting down, now and then a blinking light) in BBL Portals' "molten" texture slot, pale dust flecks, water drips and glow sparkles; the Nether whoosh is swapped for deep-sea sounds (`startup_scripts/portal_ambience.js`). The Tidecaller is a drowned compass whose needle strains upward and snaps back to the deep.

## 3. The world
- **Height and depth bands.** Ceiling underside around Y 100, seafloor between about Y −150 (shelves) and Y −480 (trench bottoms). Deep Seas measures pressure from the top of the water column, here the ceiling (to confirm in game), so depth tiers map onto its limits:
  - **Shelves and ridges (0–120 m):** reachable in plain gear. Kelp, coral, lighter water.
  - **The Plain (120–280 m):** needs the full copper diving suit. Sediment flats, glass sponges, whale falls, ruins.
  - **The Trenches (280 m and down):** submarines only (hull ratings). The biggest creatures, the boss, the best loot.
- **Biomes** (our own, under `strata:`, never in `#minecraft:is_ocean`, `#c:is_ocean` or `is_overworld`, so Overworld ocean content stays out; each mod is opted in through its own tags): Abyssal Shelf, Abyssal Plain, Hydrothermal Field (vents, brine pools, magma), Bioluminescent Garden (glowing flora, the "wonder" biome), The Trench.
- **Dimension type:** no skylight, fixed time (permanent dark, which many aquatic mobs read as night), beds work (in a dry base), compasses spin. **Sea level:** the generator's `sea_level` (568) only fills the world with water; the level itself always reports 63 in 1.21.1, and mobs check their spawn height against that. Strata Tweaks makes the level report 568 here (`LevelSeaLevelMixin`); without it almost nothing designed for this dimension spawns.
- **Terrain generator:** our own noise settings: a wide, gently rolling plain, shelves that end in a sheer drop-off (every shelf floor is within the 120 m plain gear survives), and the Trench as broad deep basins about 250 blocks across with flat floors (never through a shelf), with the ceiling as a solid cap. Built with a generator script like `tools/build_hollow.py`.
- **The ceiling problem:** most mod flora and many structures place themselves using the topmost block, which here is the ceiling. Fix: our own placed features that reuse the mods' configured features but scan down to the floor (like the Nether), and fixed-height copies of structures that need it.
- **Atmosphere:** water that gets darker with depth and is never black (the barrier here is water and air, not the dark): the biome's water fog colour gives the hue, the depth below the ceiling the brightness, 55% just under the ceiling, 30% on the plain, 16% on the trench floor (`startup_scripts/hollow_fog.js`; the game alone turns any underwater fog full-bright after a few seconds). Underwater ambience, AmbientSounds whale calls and creaks. Night vision works here.

## 4. Rules
- **The sea always wins:** in this dimension, a block broken next to water becomes water (KubeJS). The Lifepod is exempt.
- **Dry rooms need an oxygen supply** (Strata Tweaks, `AbyssalAir`). The game's own water already does the physics a base needs: a room open to the sea at the side or the top floods, and a hole in the floor does not, because water never flows up. So **moon pools and submarine docks work as built**: leave the floor open and swim up into the base, or park a submarine under it. What the game doesn't ask is where the air came from (fill a shell with blocks, dig them out, and you have a dry room for nothing). So every two seconds, for each player standing in air, the connected dry space around them is walked (the nearest 120,000 cells; a bigger space is judged on those and pays for 120,000, so no hall is too big to need oxygen). It stays dry while a Deep Seas **Oxygen Diffuser** in or next to it holds oxygen (made by an **Electrolyzer**; 2 mB plus 1 per 1,000 cells every two seconds: a full Diffuser lasts a small room about a quarter of an hour, and a 48,000-cell hall uses 25 mB a second, half of what a ship's Diffuser does). Without one the player is warned for ten seconds, then the room floods from the floor up, a layer every two seconds; pockets that can't flood (a doorway) suffocate instead. The Lifepod is exempt; ships are untouched (Deep Seas keeps their air itself). Getting the water out of a new room: a Create Hose Pulley drains a sealed room, or fill it and dig it out. `abyssalRoomsNeedOxygen` in `config/strata_tweaks-common.toml` turns the rule off.
- **Deep Seas' own rooms are ships:** its airtight compartments, breach flooding, Decompression Chamber airlock and the Diffuser's own air only work on an assembled ship (a Sable sub-level), never on blocks placed in the world. A static base runs on the rule above; a submarine runs on Deep Seas.
- **Breathing gear:** diving gear and potions only. Diving helmets with their own air supply (Create's with a backtank, Mekanism's scuba mask and MekaSuit, PneumaticCraft's helmet, Ad Astra's suits, the Hybrid Aquatic and Alex's Caves diving suits) and brewed Water Breathing work. Everything that breathes for free does not: trinkets and charms (Iron Bubble, Snorkel, Tide Ember Amulet, Ars Additions charms), armour and augments (Neptunium, Mystical Agriculture's augment, sea serpent and boss armours), pets and familiars, the turtle helmet, the Conduit, vampires. `startup_scripts/abyssal_vampire_air.js` refuses short, hidden, ambient or endless Water Breathing and Conduit Power and keeps the air bar itself for anyone without a helmet or the effect. Pressure still limits depth for everyone.
- **No Conduit power in the Abyssal Plain:** a placed Conduit would give water breathing, night vision and fast mining around a base and make the pumps, airlocks and oxygen pointless. Conduits keep working everywhere else (the effect is refused inside the dimension, with the other free ways to breathe).
- **Scary but fair:** the Hollow's damage cap and grace period apply here too, bosses excepted.

## 5. Life and content
**Mods added for this dimension:**
- **Hybrid Aquatic** (+ Hybrid API): the main kit, about 140 creatures (trench anglerfish, barreleye, dragonfish, fangtooth, colossal and vampire squid, firefly squid, yeti crab, the Karkinos miniboss) plus thermal vents, brine pools, glass sponges and whale-fall structures. Spawns follow its own biome tags, so we point the trench and vent creatures at our biomes; its Overworld biomes can be switched off in config. Its diving suits fall under the breathing-gear rule (section 4): pressure still limits them.
- **Scannable**: an FE-powered scanner, the Subnautica scanner feel.
- **Field Guide** (+ Immersive Overlays, Item Descriptions, client): scan creatures and plants with a spyglass to fill an in-game encyclopedia. Natural FTB Quests goals.
- **Sunken Spires**: 11 ruins of an ancient civilisation with minibosses and a prismarine trial chamber.
- **MOS – Moog's Ocean Structures**: Atlantis ruins, wrecks, a sea-dragon skeleton (server-only; needs fixed-height copies because of the ceiling).

**Already in the pack, moved or shared here:**
- Naturalist anglerfish and giant isopod (its `#naturalist:has_*` tags), Alex's Mobs frilled shark, giant squid and cachalot (spawn configs), Upgrade Aquatic thrasher and great thrasher plus its prismarine coral and jelly torches, Critters and Companions dumbo octopus, Regions Unexplored deep flora.
- **The boss: Cataclysm's Leviathan**, summoned in the Trench with the Leviathan Lure (section 7a). It is the Abyssal Plain's final goal, and its drops gate late tiers.

**Alex's Caves Continued: abyss content only.** Its Abyssal Chasm creatures (Deep Ones, Deep One knights and mages, the Hullbreaker, lanternfish, tripodfish) spawn only through ordinary biome spawn lists, with nothing in their code tied to its own chasm biome (checked in the 1.1.3 jar), so they and its abyssal blocks go into the Trench and Hydrothermal Field. **All six of its Overworld cave biomes are switched off** in its config (the Overworld is pregenerated, so they would only appear past 5 km with seams; Forlorn Hollows overlaps the Hollow; its submarine and diving suit overlap Deep Seas and stay unused). Cost: about 85 MB more content (RAM, startup, join time).

## 6a. Something to work toward
**The big goal: a Deep Station on the Trench floor.** Establishing a working pressure-hull base about 400+ m down, where only the best hulls hold, is the dimension's endgame. Three tracks lead there:
1. **Depth (gear):** shelves in plain gear → the Plain with the copper diving suit (120 m) → the trenches in a first submarine (280 m+) → the Trench floor with reinforced hulls. Each milestone is a quest with rewards.
2. **Boss ladder:** Karkinos (Hybrid Aquatic miniboss; it doesn't spawn naturally: you summon it by fishing with an Ominous Hook in your off hand, crafted from nine crab species' claws, anywhere with fishable water, including the Lifepod's moon pool; its claw drops wherever it dies) → the Hullbreaker (Alex's Caves, the trenches; it hunts submarines) → the **Leviathan** (Cataclysm, final boss, Sunken City at the Trench bottom). Each drops what the next step needs.
3. **The Abyssal Survey (discovery):** scan creatures and plants with the Field Guide and Scannable; completed survey sections give rewards and the story of the drowned civilisation (Sunken Spires, the Deep Ones).

**Payoff outside the dimension:** the pack rule that late Mekanism and AE2 tiers need boss drops gets one of its gates here: the Leviathan's drop plus rare **Abyssal Pearls** from the trenches. Deep-only crafting also unlocks the best hull tier and deep-dive upgrades. All of it becomes an "Abyssal Plain" FTB Quests chapter.

## 6. Rewards
- Unique materials from vents, trenches and the Leviathan, including the **Abyssal Pearl** gate item (like the Hollow's Ember).
- Per-player Lootr chests in all ruins and wrecks.
- A Field Guide / scanning quest chapter ("Abyssal Survey").

## 7a. Implementation
- **Generator:** `tools/build_abyssal_plain.py --out kubejs/data --mods <server mods folder>` writes the dimension, noise settings, biomes, features, tags, spawn wiring, structure copies and loot modifiers. Re-run after an update to Hybrid Aquatic, Alex's Caves, Sunken Spires or MOS.
- **Heights:** the column runs Y −48 to 591 because the standard noise generator hard-codes **lava below Y −54** in every dimension. Ceiling underside ~Y 560 (sea level 568), shelves ~Y 479, the plain ~Y 314, trench floors ~Y 14. Depths below the ceiling are as designed.
- **What the ceiling breaks, and the workarounds:** surface rules count depth from the topmost air and water doesn't reset that count, so seafloor sediment is laid down as disk features (clay/mud, sand/gravel, blackstone/magma, mud/moss, abyssmarine). Vanilla kelp, seagrass, sea pickles and coral find their own height from the OCEAN_FLOOR heightmap, so they're rebuilt as our own block-column/simple-block features. All decorations use down-scanning placement (`environment_scan`, max 32 blocks). `initial_density_without_jaggedness` is 0, like the Nether. Alex's Caves' boulders, tube worms and anemones don't place outside its chasm biome and are left out; its mussels, black vents, whale falls and Deep One ruins work.
- **Structures:** spaced at 40% of the table's distances (the nearest ruins were 1,000 to 4,000 blocks from the Lifepod; now 300 to 900). The terrain is far too dense for a structure's "beard" to carve, so a structure only works where the floor is already at its height: that is why the Trench is a broad flat basin (as a 50-block V the Colosseum and the Sunken City were inside solid rock). 26 copies under `strata:abyssal/` at fixed heights with `beard_box` (Sunken Spires: spires, temple, conduit ruins, spikes, fossils, forge, shrine, trial chamber buried under the plain, Colosseum and Sunken City in the trench; Moog's: Atlantis ruins and tribune, castle, church, archway, temple, wrecks, garden, magma ravine, conduit arch, dog statue, dragon skeletons in the trench). Sunken Spires exists only here (its biome tag is emptied); Moog's keeps its Overworld oceans too. Test: the Colosseum generated on the trench floor (Y 0–40).
- **The Leviathan:** Cataclysm's Sunken City picks its height in code from the ocean-floor heightmap, so it can't be placed here. The **Leviathan Lure** (6 Abyssal Pearls + Karkinos Claw + Heart of the Sea + Immortal Embryo, both boss drops guaranteed) summons the Leviathan when used in the Trench below Y 120 (one at a time).
- **Spawn hygiene (Nov 16):** mods that add spawns to every biome, or to every biome not tagged as an ocean, are removed from our biomes (`abyssal_no_strays.json`: Aquaculture's freshwater fish, Quark's stoneling and toretoise, Alex's Mobs' farseer, Mowzie's grottol, Pana's skeleton wolf). Alex's Mobs' giant squid is not added here (it piled up far past the mob cap; Hybrid Aquatic has giant and colossal squids). In Control caps per player: 4 Deep Ones, 2 knights, 1 mage, 1 Hullbreaker (the monster list holds nothing else and the water column is 500 blocks tall, so they would fill the hostile cap), 4 Naturalist anglerfish and 4 isopods (they never despawn). After a mob mod is added, check the biomes' final spawn lists for new strays.
- **Decorations go into open water only** (a `block_predicate_filter` on every floor feature): a sea pickle placed on a kelp stalk's lowest block cut the whole stalk loose.
- **Creatures:** Hybrid Aquatic's trench and vent species via its trench tags (its own Overworld biomes off), Alex's Caves' deep-sea creatures and Deep Ones and the Hullbreaker in the biome spawn lists (its six cave biomes off), Naturalist anglerfish/isopod and Upgrade Aquatic thrashers via their tags, Alex's Mobs frilled shark/giant squid/cachalot via their spawn configs.
- **Quests:** `config/ftbquests/quests/chapters/abyssal_plain.snbt`, 23 quests in the Exploration group after the Pokémon World: intro and rules, depth (shelves, diving suit, plain, first submarine, trench), base (airlock and pump, electrolyzer and oxygen diffuser), survey (Field Guide, creature observations, Scannable), ruins (Atlantis, trial chamber, Colosseum, Sunken City), bosses (Karkinos, Hullbreaker, 6 pearls, Lure, Leviathan), ending in the Deep Station. Loads clean on the test server; the observation tasks are the first in the pack, so check them in the quest editor.
- **Scripts:** `server_scripts/abyssal_rules.js` (Tidecaller and Lure recipes, Lifepod and its Return to Surface console, flood rule, no Conduit power, no sponges, Pearl drops, Lure use); the Hollow's damage cap also covers `hybrid_aquatic` and `alexscaves`; `client_scripts/strata_lang.js` names every custom dimension and biome.
- **Test server:** clean boot; 6,561-chunk sample: no air pockets, no lava (a few vent blocks), floors at design heights, sediment coverage 50–88% by biome, kelp/seagrass/coral/sea pickles/sponges/vents/brine pools present; all five biomes found only in the Abyssal Plain.
- **Tested with a client (Nov 16):** Lifepod arrival and interior, creature spawns in all five biomes, Deep Seas pressure under the ceiling (it crushes a diver in the trench), the breathing rule, the water colour by depth, the portal's sounds.
- **Also tested (Nov 16, second round, real server-side clicks and breaks):** the Return to Surface console, the flood rule, the dry-room rule (a dug-out pocket floods after the warning; a room with a supplied Diffuser stays dry and uses its oxygen; a moon pool holds; a side breach floods), the Lure (it summons the Leviathan and is used up), the capsule Lifepod on a shelf at 76 m, Atlantis ruins and a castle on the plain floor, the Colosseum in open water on the trench floor.
- **Not yet tested:** an Electrolyzer feeding a Diffuser through pipes in a static base, a Hose Pulley draining a room, a real submarine docking under a moon pool, the Leviathan fight itself, the Sunken City up close (in one test world a trench wall still covered part of it).

## 7. Build steps
1. Add the mods; set Hybrid Aquatic's Overworld biomes off and retarget its tags.
2. `tools/build_abyssal_plain.py`: dimension, ceilinged noise settings, biomes, tags, down-scanning placed features, structure copies.
3. KubeJS: portal and Tidecaller, Lifepod generation and protection, the flood rule, damage cap/grace, no Conduit power, no sponges.
4. Test server: leak check (nothing aquatic-only escapes, no Overworld ocean content inside), spawn counts per biome, structures land on the floor.
5. **Playtest with clients:** Lifepod arrival, a pumped-dry hull base on the seafloor, pressure bands, submarine dive to the Trench, the Leviathan.
6. Pregenerate on the server (taller dimension, so roughly 1.7× the Hollow's generation time).
