# The Hollow: horror mod research

Research for `strata:hollow` (our own datapack dimension, entered by portal). Goal: psychological dread first, some combat horror, unique loot, and **horror mobs only inside the Hollow**.

Method:
- Builds come from the Modrinth API (`/v2/project/<slug>/version?loaders=["neoforge"]&game_versions=["1.21.1"]`). CurseForge was checked through cfwidget for the projects that are not on Modrinth for 1.21.1.
- Spawn behaviour comes from the jars themselves. The jars were downloaded to a scratch directory (one folder per mod) and read there: biome modifier JSON, biome tags, config class strings, and `javap` disassembly of the spawn-condition classes. Nothing from a jar was run.

Already in the pack: **In Control! 10.3.0** (`mods/in-control.pw.toml`, no config written yet), KubeJS, AmbientSounds, Sound Physics Remastered, Vanilla Backport, Regions Unexplored, Vampirism, plus geckolib, fzzy-config, puzzles-lib, terrablender, yacl, balm, azurelib, blueprint, curios, patchouli and simple-voice-chat. None of the horror candidates below is in the pack yet.

Spawn method key:
- **a**: NeoForge biome modifier (datapack JSON or biome tag). We override it so it targets Hollow biomes only.
- **b**: the mod's own spawn logic, with a dimension whitelist or blacklist in its config.
- **c**: the mod's own spawn logic with no dimension control. It needs In Control!, KubeJS, or it can't be used.
- **d**: ambient or event-only (no mobs).

## Key findings that shape the design

1. **Many MCreator mods hard-code the Overworld in their spawn rules.** Their biome modifiers say `neoforge:any`, but the entity's spawn-placement predicate checks `level.dimension() == Level.OVERWORLD` (confirmed in bytecode). So pointing their biome modifiers at Hollow biomes does nothing, because the predicate still rejects every spawn there. Affected mods: Born in Chaos (all 23 spawn-condition procedures), ZoNiEx (Overworld, or Nether if `ADD_TO_NETHER` is on), Skinwalker Hunt, Midnight Lurker, The Obsessed, The Knocker, and ArPhEx (unless its "Spawn in other dimensions" toggle is on). To get these mobs into the Hollow you would have to skip the vanilla spawner: either a KubeJS ticking spawner that calls `EntityType.spawn` near players in `strata:hollow`, or an In Control! `spawner.json` rule. Whether In Control!'s spawner honours the mob's own spawn predicate is unverified and needs a test.
2. **Hollow biomes must be unique to the Hollow and must not carry `#minecraft:is_overworld` or `#c:is_overworld` / `#c:in_overworld`.** Vampirism's vampire, hunter and dungeon spawns all target `#minecraft:is_overworld` (checked in its jar), and many other mods do the same. If the Hollow reused an Overworld biome such as `vanillabackport:pale_garden` or `regions_unexplored:blackwood_taiga`, two things would go wrong: every Overworld mob mod would spawn in the Hollow, and any horror spawn we add to that biome would also spawn in the Overworld. So copy the biome JSONs into the `strata:` namespace (for example `strata:hollow_pale_garden`, which can keep the creaking-heart features) and tag them `#strata:hollow_biomes` only.
3. **The vanilla datapack already covers most of the atmosphere, with no mod.** Biome `effects` give fog, sky and water-fog colour, `ambient_sound` loops, `mood_sound`, `additions_sound`, `particle` and `music`. The dimension type gives `ambient_light: 0`, `has_skylight: false` or `has_ceiling: true`, `fixed_time`, `effects` and `monster_spawn_light_level`. AmbientSounds (already in the pack) can add dimension- or biome-conditioned soundscapes through a resource pack. KubeJS can apply Darkness or Blindness pulses only while a player is in `strata:hollow`.
4. **Safety net.** Add an In Control! `spawn.json` rule that denies every horror mod's namespace outside `strata:hollow`. That catches anything a mod adds later. Check the exact keys (`mod`, `dimension`, `result: deny`) against the In Control! docs before writing it.

## Recommended set (8 mods plus things already in the pack)

1. **Cave Dweller ReEvolved**: the core dread stalker, with a real dimension whitelist (b). Flag: beta only.
2. **Weeping Angels**: "don't look away" dread, controlled with a tag override and the blacklist. Turn off interdimensional teleport and snow angels, and override the catacombs tag.
3. **Whispering Spirits**: cheap ambient presence, one JSON override.
4. **Sanity: Renewed**: the psychological layer (meter, hallucinations, inner entities), Hollow-only through its per-dimension file. It needs the most config work and a client test.
5. **The Ravenous**: chase-horror bridge between dread and combat. Two JSON overrides.
6. **Grim & Bleak**: combat mobs, structures and unique gear and ores, all on biomes the Overworld never uses. Put them into the Hollow biome source, and block its Overworld portal ruins and Soulsrealm entry.
7. **The Graveyard 4.0**: undead, crypts and a boss with loot. All spawns and structures are tag-driven. Disable hordes. If 4.0 proves unstable, use the unofficial port 2.6.2 instead.
8. **Mutant Monsters**: rare mini-bosses. Set config weights to 0 and add our own Hollow spawn JSON.

Optional: **True Darkness Biomes** (client darkness for Hollow biomes only; old beta) and **Nyctophobia** (biome palette to copy).

Why this set: every mob source in it can be fenced to Hollow biomes with JSON, tags or a dimension whitelist, and none of them makes world-wide events, griefs bases, or hard-codes the Overworld. The dread comes from Cave Dweller, the Angels, the spirits, Sanity, plus our own fog, sound and darkness. Ravenous, Grim & Bleak, Graveyard and the mutants supply combat and the unique loot (Grim & Bleak darksteel and red amber gear, Graveyard boss drops, mutant drops, plus our own Lootr loot tables in Hollow structures).

Build notes for phase 2:
- Datapack overrides: write a file at each mod's biome-modifier path (or tag path) that targets `#strata:hollow_biomes`. Tag overrides need `"replace": true`.
- In Control! `spawn.json`: deny the namespaces `cave_dweller`, `weeping_angels`, `whispering_spirits`, `the_ravenous`, `grim_and_bleak`, `graveyard`, `mutantmonsters` and `sanity_renewed` outside `strata:hollow`.
- These mobs keep their designed stats, the same treatment bosses get. There is no server-wide staged difficulty.

## Rejected (one line each)

- The Knocker, The Obsessed, Skinwalker Hunt, Midnight Lurker NF, ZoNiEx, Born in Chaos: spawn rules hard-code the Overworld (or the Overworld and Nether).
- Kenny, The Boy and the Bath, Him, Face of Horror, Door_Knocker: no dimension control. Events follow the player everywhere, and Kenny griefs bases.
- Server-Side Horror: world-wide fake joins, torch breaking and house burning, with no dimension filter.
- Hollow Steve: its encounters only run in the Overworld. Maybe later as a summoned boss.
- ArPhEx: huge, has its own dimensions and an omnipresent teleporting boss. Hard to contain.
- Fungal Infection: Spore: spreading block and mob infection that can leak through portals.
- Imposter / Mimicked: voice mimicry is global by design.
- Backwoods, Backrooms, The Afterdark (1.1.0 **alpha**): separate horror dimensions that compete with the Hollow.
- From The Fog: no NeoForge 1.21.1 mod (only a datapack), and it is global.
- More Darkness: no per-dimension control.
- Gigeresque (CC-BY-NC-ND), Warlery's Dark Blood (CC-BY-ND, global nights): restrictive licenses or global effects.
- No 1.21.1 build: The Man From The Fog, Cave Dweller Evolved and the original Cave Dweller, Whisperwoods, Sanity: Descent into Madness, Paranoia, The Mimicer, The Irritator.
- Horror Messages, The Null (beta), Broken Script Additions (beta), Warning, Horror Faces, End Watchers: chat or gimmick mods, betas, or ones that target the End or every dimension. Not inspected further.
